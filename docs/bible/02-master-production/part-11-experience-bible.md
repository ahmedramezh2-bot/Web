# EXPERIENCE BIBLE • BUSINESS • SERVICES • CLIENT JOURNEY

====================================================

IMPORTANT

Visitors never come to HEBRA to buy.

They come to discover.

Business happens because emotion created trust.

Never interrupt curiosity with sales.

Never destroy immersion with marketing.

Never break the world.

====================================================

THE EXPERIENCE

The visitor begins with mystery.

Leaves with clarity.

The journey should feel inevitable.

Never forced.

ANALYTICS

Measure quietly.

Never optimize for clicks.

Curiosity.

Chapter completion.

Interaction depth.

Return visits.

====================================================

SKIP SYSTEM

The cinematic introduction may be skipped.

Visitors control their experience.

Rules:

Skip becomes available after the first meaningful reveal.

Returning visitors may begin where they left off.

Remember progress locally.

Never force repeated introductions.

Respect the visitor's time.

====================================================

RETURN VISITS

Returning visitors should discover change.

Tiny environmental evolution.

Seasonal variation.

Hidden discoveries.

Subtle narrative growth.

The world continues living.

====================================================

ACCESSIBILITY

Accessibility never means a simplified identity.

Create parallel interaction paths.

Not inferior experiences.

Reduced Motion.

Keyboard Navigation.

Screen Reader Support.

High Contrast.

Adaptive Audio.

All preserve the emotional language.

====================================================

RESPONSIVE EXPERIENCE

Desktop remains the reference experience.

High-end mobile devices should preserve approximately 90–95% of the artistic experience.

Never create a "mobile version."

Adapt quality.

Never adapt identity.

Allow minor performance tradeoffs when necessary to preserve atmosphere.

Reduce:

Particle count.

Shadow resolution.

Reflection samples.

Shader iterations.

Never remove:

Core shaders.

World layout.

Story chapters.

Volumetric identity.

Material language.

but keeps thinking about it all day...

The experience succeeded.

Business follows unforgettable experiences.

Not the other way around.
