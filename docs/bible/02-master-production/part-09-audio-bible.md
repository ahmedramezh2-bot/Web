# HEBRA MASTER PRODUCTION BIBLE

## PART 9

# AUDIO BIBLE • MUSIC • ATMOSPHERE • EMOTIONAL SOUND DESIGN

====================================================

IMPORTANT

Sound is not decoration.

Sound is architecture.

Visitors should feel HEBRA before they understand it.

Every sound exists to reinforce emotion.

Never fill silence.

Never fear silence.

Silence is part of the composition.

====================================================

THE PURPOSE OF AUDIO

Audio exists to create presence.

Not excitement.

Not entertainment.

Presence.

The visitor should constantly feel:

Something is alive.

Something ancient is watching.

Something impossible exists beyond the visible world.

====================================================

THE SOUND LANGUAGE

HEBRA has one sound language.

No UI sounds.

No generic hover effects.

No notification sounds.

No game sounds.

Everything belongs to one civilization.

====================================================

MUSIC

Music should evolve continuously.

Never obvious loops.

Never repetitive melodies.

Never commercial music.

Never trailer music.

Never cinematic clichés.

Instead...

Slow evolution.

Massive atmosphere.

Organic harmony.

Patience.

Mystery.

Hope.

Scale.

====================================================

INSPIRATION

Emotionally study works similar in feeling to:

Interstellar

Arrival

Blade Runner 2049

Dune

The Tree of Life

Annihilation

WITHOUT copying.

Extract emotional principles.

Never melodies.

Never orchestration.

Never recognizable motifs.

====================================================

TONE.JS

Tone.js owns procedural audio.

Responsibilities:

Ambient layers.

Harmonic reactions.

Touch resonance.

Environmental evolution.

Interactive sound design.

Never use it to imitate orchestral scores.

====================================================

SPATIAL AUDIO

Every location owns its own atmosphere.

Different architecture.

Different resonance.

Different frequencies.

Different silence.

Moving through the world changes the soundscape naturally.

====================================================

TOUCH

Every touch produces consequence.

Glass.

Crystal.

Stone.

Metal.

Energy.

Each material owns its own voice.

No generic tap sound.

====================================================

MOUSE

Mouse movement should barely influence ambience.

Tiny harmonic shifts.

Tiny particle resonance.

Tiny environmental breathing.

Almost subconscious.

====================================================

SCROLL

Movement changes atmosphere.

Never instantly.

Layers slowly evolve.

The visitor should feel like travelling through climates.

Not playlists.

====================================================

ARCHITECTURE

Large spaces sound large.

Small spaces sound intimate.

Open environments breathe.

Hidden chambers whisper.

Distance matters.

====================================================

SILENCE

Silence is sacred.

Never constantly play music.

Moments without music often create stronger emotion.

====================================================

INTERACTION

Every interaction belongs to one audio language.

Buttons.

Artifacts.

Architecture.

Glyphs.

Everything sounds related.

====================================================

PROCEDURAL PHILOSOPHY

The soundtrack should never play exactly the same way twice.

Tiny procedural variation.

Pitch.

Timing.

Layer activation.

Ambient modulation.

Keep the experience alive.

====================================================

VOICE

Avoid narration.

Avoid exposition.

Avoid talking.

If voices exist...

Treat them like memories.

Almost impossible to understand.

Emotion first.

Meaning second.

====================================================

REVERB

Space defines reverb.

Not presets.

Every environment owns its own acoustic identity.

====================================================

DYNAMIC RANGE

Protect dynamic range.

Allow quiet moments.

Allow loud moments.

Never compress everything.

Contrast creates emotion.

====================================================

FINAL TEST

Mute the visuals.

Listen only.

Can someone still feel HEBRA?

If not...

Redesign the sound.

====================================================

DO NOT IMPLEMENT.

Internalize the sound philosophy.

Wait for Part 10.
