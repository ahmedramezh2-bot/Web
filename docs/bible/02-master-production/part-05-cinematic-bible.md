# HEBRA MASTER PRODUCTION BIBLE

## PART 5

# CINEMATIC BIBLE • CAMERA • PACING • DIRECTION

====================================================

IMPORTANT

HEBRA is not viewed.

HEBRA is experienced.

The visitor should never feel they are controlling a webpage.

The visitor should feel like they are inside a film that quietly reacts to them.

The camera is invisible.

Its existence should never be noticed.

====================================================

THE DIRECTOR

Theatre.js is the Film Director.

Every important movement is intentional.

Every second has purpose.

Nothing moves because it can.

Everything moves because the story demands it.

====================================================

CAMERA PHILOSOPHY

The camera is not an observer.

The camera is consciousness.

It breathes.

It hesitates.

It anticipates.

It discovers.

It remembers.

Never use robotic movement.

Never use game camera movement.

Never use obvious keyframes.

Everything must feel naturally alive.

====================================================

CAMERA MOVEMENT

Camera movement should feel:

Heavy.

Elegant.

Confident.

Ancient.

Patient.

Weightless without feeling unrealistic.

====================================================

NO SUDDEN MOVEMENT

Avoid:

Hard cuts.

Instant rotations.

Fast zooms.

Abrupt direction changes.

Camera shakes without purpose.

Every movement should transition naturally.

====================================================

CAMERA RAILS

The visitor influences movement.

The visitor never completely controls movement.

Camera rails define the emotional journey.

Mouse.

Touch.

Scroll.

All influence the camera.

None completely override it.

====================================================

FRAMING

Every frame should be beautiful enough to become a wallpaper.

Every composition should feel intentional.

Use:

Negative space.

Depth.

Silhouettes.

Leading lines.

Scale.

Atmospheric perspective.

Never place important elements randomly.

====================================================

PACE

Silence has value.

Stillness has value.

Do not fear slow moments.

The visitor should have time to observe.

Time to think.

Time to wonder.

====================================================

VISUAL RHYTHM

Alternate between:

Large spaces.

Intimate spaces.

Darkness.

Light.

Silence.

Music.

Movement.

Stillness.

Contrast creates rhythm.

====================================================

THE REVEAL

Never reveal everything.

Reveal layers.

The world rewards patience.

Visitors who stay longer discover more.

Visitors who explore deeper discover hidden meaning.

====================================================

TRANSITIONS

Transitions are physical.

Reality shifts.

Architecture opens.

Light transforms.

The camera continues moving.

Avoid fades whenever possible.

Avoid black screens except when emotionally necessary.

====================================================

DEPTH

Every scene should have:

Foreground.

Midground.

Background.

Atmospheric depth.

Nothing should feel flat.

====================================================

FOCUS

Guide attention naturally.

Never force it.

Use:

Light.

Movement.

Sound.

Scale.

Composition.

Instead of arrows.

Instead of instructions.

====================================================

MOMENTS OF WONDER

Every chapter should contain at least one unforgettable cinematic moment.

Not because it is technically impressive.

Because it creates emotion.

====================================================

THE VISITOR

Never rush the visitor.

Never pressure them.

Never overwhelm them.

The visitor should feel respected.

====================================================

UI

The interface is part of the film.

It appears naturally.

It disappears naturally.

It never interrupts immersion.

====================================================

SOUND SYNCHRONIZATION

Camera movement.

Music.

Ambient sound.

Particles.

Lighting.

Everything belongs to one rhythm.

Nothing should feel disconnected.

====================================================

NO EMPTY CINEMA

Beautiful shots are meaningless without purpose.

Every camera move must answer:

Why is this happening?

If no answer exists...

Remove it.

====================================================

FINAL TEST

Pause the experience at any random second.

Would this frame look like it belongs to a premium cinematic production?

If not...

Redesign the composition.

====================================================

DO NOT IMPLEMENT.

Understand the cinematic language.

Wait for Part 6.
