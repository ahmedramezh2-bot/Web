# HEBRA PRODUCTION ROADMAP

Before writing or modifying a single line of code, read this entire roadmap carefully.

This roadmap is AUTHORITATIVE.

Do not skip phases.

Do not merge phases.

Do not reorder phases.

Do not improvise your own production order.

Each phase must be completed, reviewed, optimized and approved before moving to the next one.

After every phase perform:

• Creative Review
• Engineering Review
• Performance Review
• Accessibility Review
• Identity Review

PHASE 5 — ENVIRONMENTS

Goal:

Build every Physical Location (Level 2) and Environmental Dialect (Level 3), per the Canonical Naming Architecture (docs/bible/26).

[Terminology note: this phase originally listed eight names — Workshop, Archive, Temple, Library, Observatory, Creation Chamber, Memory Hall, Origin Core — as if they were the locations themselves. Per docs/bible/26, these eight are the Environmental Dialects (Level 3, now renamed The Active/Hushed/Reverent/Attentive/Vast/Generative/Recollective/Genesis Dialects) — reusable design languages, not locations. This phase builds both: the actual Level 2 Physical Locations (The Starfield Reach, The Cosmic Dust Field, The Monument Range, The Sentinel Ring, The Crystal Garden, The Energy Stream, The Origin Core, The Distant Heart, The Rising Gate, and the six Discipline Zones), each rendered using one or more of the eight Dialects per docs/bible/26 §3's table.]

Exit Criteria:

Every Physical Location has unique identity (World Blueprint §7); every Environmental Dialect is distinct and reusable across more than one Location.
