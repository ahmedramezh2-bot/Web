====================================================

## 1. WHY THIS REPORT EXISTS

The Phase Gate Review (`docs/bible/23-phase-gate-review.md`) identified a four-scheme location-naming fragmentation as the sole remaining blocker to full Production Authorization, and proposed a layered resolution. The Creative Director approved that resolution and directed a Constitution-wide refactor with three explicit requirements: (1) implement the four-level architecture without simply renaming things, (2) search for and resolve every other terminology conflict found in the process, and (3) produce this report cataloging exactly what changed.

All three requirements are addressed below.

====================================================

## 2. THE NEW CANONICAL ARCHITECTURE

`docs/bible/26-canonical-naming-architecture.md` establishes four permanent levels:

- **Level 1 — Story Chapters:** Void, Monument, Fragments, Origin, Awakening, Threshold. (Unchanged — never actually in conflict.)
- **Level 2 — Physical Locations:** the real, traversable places — nine pre-Origin locations (The Starfield Reach, The Cosmic Dust Field, The Monument Range, The Sentinel Ring, The Crystal Garden, The Energy Stream, The Origin Core, The Distant Heart, The Rising Gate) plus six post-Origin Discipline Zones (The Creation Zone, The Identity Zone, The Architecture Zone, The Technology Zone, The Imagination Zone, The Legacy Zone).
- **Level 3 — Environmental Dialects:** eight reusable mood/lighting/material/audio languages, renamed from their original place-shaped names to mood-shaped names specifically so they can never again be mistaken for locations: The Attentive, Reverent, Active, Vast, Hushed, Genesis, Generative, and Recollective Dialects.
- **Level 4 — Business Destinations:** Website Design, Brand Identity, Interactive Experiences, AI Systems, Career Boost, About, Contact, Projects — each fixed to a specific hosting Discipline Zone.

Every Dialect renaming preserves its underlying content exactly — no artistic decision already made in the Lighting, Material, FX, or Audio Production Bibles was altered. Only the label changed.

## 6. OTHER CONFLICTS RESOLVED IN THIS PASS

Per the Creative Director's instruction to resolve every conflict found, not only the naming one:

- **The "World" taxonomy overload** — Part 3's "only ONE world exists" versus World Blueprint's prior use of "World" as a sub-region taxonomy term. Resolved: "World" is now reserved exclusively for the single, singular explorable space; sub-regions use "Path," "Zone," or "Location."
- **Chunk Loading vs. "never unload it."** — never formally reconciled before. Resolved in doc 26 §7: Chunk Loading manages rendering detail only, never narrative continuity; the world is never unloaded or replaced from the visitor's experiential point of view even where its rendering detail streams.
- **"Discipline room" vs "Discipline Zone."** — five separate documents used the informal term. Resolved: "Discipline Zone" is now the sole canonical term, used consistently across the Camera Bible, World Blueprint, UI Integration Bible, Development Standards, the Technical Addendum, and the Production Roadmap.

No new conflicts beyond these three and the core naming fragmentation were identified during this pass. The tool-responsibility table, the Blender export pipeline, and the Part 6 truncation were already resolved by Development Standards prior to this refactor and required no further action.
