# HEBRA MASTER PRODUCTION BIBLE

## THE CANONICAL NAMING ARCHITECTURE

*Documentation only. Pre-production. Nothing in this document is implemented by its own existence.*

====================================================

PREAMBLE

This document exists to end an ambiguity that had been quietly accumulating since before this session began: HEBRA's locations, moods, and business content had four different, non-identical naming schemes describing overlapping territory, none of which formally deferred to any other.

This document does not simply rename things. It establishes a permanent, four-level architecture in which every future name in HEBRA belongs to exactly one level, and no two levels may ever be confused for each other again. Every other document in this Constitution that touches naming is now read through this one.

Where this document is silent, the Creative Director Protocol (Appendix G) governs. This document was authorized directly by the Creative Director in response to the Phase Gate Review's §5 finding (`docs/bible/23-phase-gate-review.md`), and per that authorization it now supersedes every prior, informal naming scheme in the Constitution.

====================================================

## 1. THE FOUR LEVELS

**LEVEL 1 — STORY CHAPTERS.** The narrative progression only. Six chapters: Void, Monument, Fragments, Origin, Awakening, Threshold. This level answers one question: *where is the visitor in the story?* It has no spatial, material, or commercial content of its own — it is pure narrative sequence, owned by the Camera Bible (Camera Bible §4) and, once written, the future Story Bible.

**LEVEL 2 — PHYSICAL LOCATIONS.** The actual places the visitor travels through. This level answers: *where is the visitor in space?* Every Physical Location belongs to exactly one Story Chapter (pre-Origin) or to the post-Origin functional layer, and is a real, traversable, uniquely-silhouetted place per World Blueprint §7. Owned by the World Blueprint.

**LEVEL 3 — ENVIRONMENTAL DIALECTS.** Reusable artistic languages that define mood, lighting, materials, shaders, audio, atmosphere, particles, and architectural behavior. **These are not locations.** They are reusable design languages. A single Physical Location may use one or multiple Dialects. Owned jointly by the Lighting, Material, FX, and Audio Production Bibles, each of which reads the same eight Dialects and applies its own discipline's treatment to them.

**LEVEL 4 — BUSINESS DESTINATIONS.** The actual functional HEBRA spaces (Services, Career Boost, Contact, About, Projects) which naturally emerge inside the world. This level answers: *what commercial or functional purpose does the visitor find here?* A Business Destination always emerges inside a Level 2 Physical Location — it is never a location in its own right, and it is never a Dialect. Owned by the UI Integration Bible §5 and Part 11.

**The governing rule:** a name that describes *when* in the story belongs to Level 1. A name that describes *where* the visitor physically is belongs to Level 2. A name that describes *what mood/register* a place is rendered in belongs to Level 3. A name that describes *what the visitor can do or get* belongs to Level 4. No name may answer more than one of these four questions at once.

A Dialect is never itself traversable — a visitor does not "go to" The Reverent Dialect. They go to The Monument Range, which is *rendered in* The Reverent Dialect.

"Environment" remains acceptable as an ordinary, lowercase, common-noun word (e.g., "environmental storytelling," "environmental awareness," "environment map") — this restriction applies only to its use as a proper-noun taxonomy term naming a specific place or mood.

## 7. OTHER TERMINOLOGY CONFLICTS RESOLVED IN THIS PASS

Per the Creative Director's instruction to resolve every conflict found while performing this refactor, not only the naming one:

- **"World" overload.** Part 3 states "only ONE world exists — never unload it, never replace it" while World Blueprint §4 used "World" as a taxonomy term for sub-regions (Main World, Secondary Worlds, Hidden Worlds). Resolved per the glossary in §6 — "World" is now reserved exclusively for the single, singular explorable space; every sub-region uses "Path," "Zone," or "Location" instead, never "World."
- **Chunk Loading vs. "never unload it."** World Blueprint §10's Chunk Loading strategy and Part 3's "never unload it, never replace it, never fake scene transitions" rule were never formally reconciled. Resolved: Chunk Loading manages *rendering detail* (LOD, streaming) per World Blueprint §10, never *narrative continuity* — the world is never unloaded or replaced from the visitor's experiential point of view even where its rendering detail streams in and out; this distinction is now explicit rather than merely implied.
- **Tool-responsibility restatement count.** Already resolved by Development Standards §6 (Phase Gate Review §2) — noted here only for completeness, no further action needed.
- **"Discipline room" vs "Discipline Zone."** Resolved per §3 and §6 — every future reference uses "Discipline Zone."

## 8. OWNERSHIP AND AMENDMENT

This document is now the single source of truth for Level 1–4 naming. No other document may introduce a new Physical Location, Environmental Dialect, or Business Destination name without first registering it here, per the same amendment discipline the Camera Bible established for its own load-bearing sections (Camera Bible §26) — a new name is a constitutional change, not a local one.

Any future new Story Chapter, Physical Location, Dialect, or Business Destination is added to the relevant table in §2–§5 before it is used anywhere else in the Constitution.

====================================================

## 9. NAMING OATH

I know which of the four questions I answer: when, where, in what mood, or for what purpose. I never answer two at once.

I am never a location pretending to be a mood, and never a mood pretending to be a location.

I am registered here before I appear anywhere else.

I do not reuse a name another level has already claimed.

Every future hand that names something in HEBRA inherits this oath before it inherits any label.
